What it is

One job: are dogs at this park. The list shows a count, not names. Still there after 90 minutes? Check in again.

One Cloudflare host. Each park is its own room of 80 check-ins. Check-in lasts 90 minutes, then it is erased. No GPS track, photos, or dog names on the occupancy wire. Park list stays without Keep.

Keep

Paid download $0.99. Keep is a 30-day free trial, then $0.99/month through Apple. Park list stays without Keep. Check-in waits on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, check-in ends with it. Restore on the Keep screen. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.

Location is When In Use, for nearby parks. Deny it and a short list still works. No background tracking. No account. No chat.
