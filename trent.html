Trent: Trades Prep

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Learn the concepts behind HVAC, plumbing, and electrical work in short lessons and quizzes built for adults.

Trent is a study tool. It is not a credential, license, or certification, and it is not a substitute for hands-on training.

Pick a trade and work through a chapter at your own pace. Each lesson is short, and a quiz helps you check what stuck.

Your progress

Your progress travels with you. Trent saves your profile, study progress, and streak in iCloud Key-Value Storage with your Apple account, so it comes back on a new phone signed in to the same Apple account. Apple stores this; it is not sent to Night Orders. The app also keeps a local copy on this phone for speed.

There is no Night Orders account or password. No ads. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Keep

Keep is an optional subscription through Apple, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers. It auto-renews until you cancel; cancel in Apple Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Restore purchases on the Keep screen. Your progress is not deleted when Keep ends.

Learning only

Trent explains concepts and safety. Electrical, gas, refrigerant, and plumbing work can injure or kill and is regulated where you live. Hire or become a licensed professional, and follow your local code. Codes vary by place and change over time.

Trent is for adults and is available in the United States.
