2. On your device

Your progress stays on this device. On this device the app stores your progress, XP, rank and streak, and whether you have seen the first-open note. The first-open note collects no data.

Your progress is not deleted when Keep ends.

3. Network

No account. The app makes no network requests of its own; purchases go through Apple.

The Privacy Policy, Support and Terms of Use links in the app open in your browser.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares with us the feedback, screenshots, and crash reports you choose to send, along with basic device details. We use them only to fix the app.

4. Keep and purchases

Keep is an optional subscription. Purchases go through Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the free trial or the current period ends.

Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

5. We do not

  • Use accounts
  • Use ads, analytics SDKs or tracking SDKs
  • Collect your age, employer, license number or trade status

6. Adults

Wrench & Wire is for adults. It is not in the App Store Kids category.

7. Learning only

For learning only. This app explains concepts and safety. It is not a license, certification, or substitute for hands-on training. Electrical, gas, refrigerant, and plumbing work can injure or kill and is regulated where you live. Hire or become a licensed professional, and follow your local code. Codes vary by place and change over time.
