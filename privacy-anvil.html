Stellar Anvil privacy

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Stellar Anvil is a paid App Store download from Night Orders. No Night Orders account. No ads. No analytics. No chat. No location. No in-app purchases.

Your forge is saved on your device and in iCloud Key-Value Storage with your Apple account, so it comes back on a new device signed in to the same Apple account. Apple stores that copy; it is not sent to us.

AccountYour Apple account only, for iCloud travel. No Night Orders account or password.
ProgressOn your device, and in iCloud Key-Value Storage with your Apple account: forge, Chronicle reveals, and patron and skin flags. If iCloud is off or you are signed out, progress stays on the device only. An empty iCloud copy never erases the forge on your device.
Export / ImportExport makes a file of your forge that you move yourself. Import replaces this device’s forge from that file. The file goes only where you send it.
Our serversNone hold your progress. We cannot restore it from our side; we never have it.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used.

The App Store sale goes through Apple. Apple’s privacy terms apply to that transaction.
