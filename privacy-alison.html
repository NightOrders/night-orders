Alison privacy

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Alison is a personal headache journal from Night Orders. Your journal stays device and account only. No Night Orders password. No ads. No analytics. No chat. Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. You can override any hint and delete any row.

AccountYour Apple ID only, for private iCloud when sync is available. No Night Orders password.
JournalDevice and account only. Export/Import first. Optional private iCloud for the same Apple ID when sync is available. Night Orders does not keep your journal as a studio cloud store. Deleting the app without a backup can remove rows on that device.
Export / ImportMove your journal between devices you control. Prefer Export/Import before relying on sync.
Private iCloudWhen sync is available. Same Apple ID. Private to you. Night Orders does not read other users’ private stores.
Apple WatchOptional. Start and intensity on the paired devices only.
Apple HealthOptional. Alison can read food, water, and sleep already in Apple Health on that device. Deny still works. Alison does not write diagnoses to Health. HealthKit / Health samples are never uploaded to Night Orders cloud. Each device re-reads Health after you grant.
Open Food FactsLive search when you look up a food. Optional camera barcode. The photo is not saved. Deny still works. You can type the code.
LocationWhen In Use, optional. Weather, pollen, and pressure only. No Always. Journal still works if denied.
NotesOff by default. Warn-only when the same things have shown up together three times. Not a medical alarm. Not a forecast. No daily nag.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.
Keep30-day free trial, then $0.99 / month through Apple. Journal readable without Keep. New Start, Food, and Health pull wait on Keep. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.

Optional Notes look at diary facts you already logged—foods, weather, sleep, meds, environment—and only after the same pairing shows up three times. That is what has shown up together, not a forecast. Those observations stay device and account only.
