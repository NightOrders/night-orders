Alison privacy

The record stays with you.

Effective 26 August 2026. Night Orders. Independent developer. Bundle com.alison.app. Live page: https://nightorders.app/privacy-alison.html

Alison is an on-device headache journal. Rows stay on your device. No account. No ads. No analytics SDK. No chat. Alison is not a medical device. Not ICHD-3. Health is a pipe. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. You can override any hint and delete any row.

AccountNone. Not required.
JournalOn your device. Not sent to Night Orders. Deleting the app deletes the journal. We cannot restore it.
Apple WatchOptional. Start and intensity on the paired devices only.
Apple HealthOptional read of food, water, and sleep already on the device. Deny still works. Alison does not write diagnoses to Health.
Open Food FactsLive search when you look up a food. Optional camera barcode. The photo is not saved. Deny still works. You can type the code.
NotesOff by default. Warn-only for what has shown up together. Three repeats. Not a medical alarm. Not a forecast. No daily nag. You can override any hint and delete any row.
LocationWhen In Use, optional. Weather, pollen, and pressure only. No Always. Journal still works if denied.
KeepPaid download $0.99. 30-day free trial, then $0.99 / month through Apple. Journal readable without Keep. New Start, Food, and Health pull wait on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, those services end. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. GDPR / CCPA: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.

Observational pairs stay on your device. Foods, weather, sleep, meds, env. Three repeats. They are diary facts: what has shown up together, not a forecast.

Support: support@nightorders.app.
