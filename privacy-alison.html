Alison privacy

Effective 25 August 2026. Night Orders. Independent developer. Bundle com.alison.app.

Alison is a personal headache journal. Rows stay on your device. No account. No ads. No analytics SDK. No chat. Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition.

AccountNone. Not required.
JournalOn your device. Not sent to Night Orders.
Apple WatchOptional. Start and intensity on the paired devices only.
Apple HealthOptional read of food, water, and sleep already on the device. Turn On All if you want that overlay. Deny still works. Alison does not write diagnoses to Health.
LocationWhen In Use, optional. Weather, pollen, and pressure only. Journal still works if denied.
Keep30-day free trial, then $0.99 / month through Apple. Journal readable without Keep. New Start, Food, and Health pull wait on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, those services end. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
Tracking / ads / IDFANot used.

Observational pairs stay on your device. They are diary facts, not a forecast.

Deleting the app removes the local journal. We cannot restore it.

Future support: nightorders.apps@icloud.com. The inbox is not live. Mint is 30 August 2026.
