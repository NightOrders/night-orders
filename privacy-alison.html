Alison privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-09.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your journal stays on your device.

Alison has no account and no Alison server. Your journal, food log, symptom entries and photos stay on your device, and we do not collect them. We do not sell your data, use it for ads, or track you across apps or sites. Depending on your Alison version: Alison 1.1 (current App Store) may send your location to weather and pollen services (coordinates, and in the US a ZIP code) and food search terms to a food database; Alison 1.2 may send an approximate location, rounded to about 11 km, directly from your phone to MET Norway in Oslo, Norway, for a weather forecast, and food search terms to a food database—optional weather from MET Norway may be unavailable. We do not link this to your identity.

Alison is a personal headache journal from Night Orders. It is not a medical device. It is not a diagnosis. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition. You can override any hint and delete any row.

AccountNone. Alison has no sign-up, no Night Orders password, and no Alison server.

Weather and location

Alison 1.1 (current App Store): Optional. Alison asks for location When In Use, and uses it when you tap weather. No Always. When you do, the phone sends its coordinates to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com and air-quality-api.open-meteo.com) for pressure, humidity, air quality, and pollen. It sends the same coordinates to OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to look up your place and ZIP code. In the US and its territories, Alison then sends that 5-digit ZIP code, not the coordinates, to pollen.com (www.pollen.com) for a pollen index. A ZIP code is coarse location. Those services see your network address and the coordinates or ZIP code, and handle them under their own policies. Night Orders does not receive these lookups. No journal rows are sent. Deny location and the journal still works.

Alison 1.2: Optional. Alison asks for location While Using the App, and uses it only when you tap weather. No Always. When you do, your phone rounds its coordinates to about 0.1 degree (about 11 km / 7 miles) and sends them directly to MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) in Oslo, Norway, at api.met.no, to get a forecast of pressure and humidity. There is no Night Orders proxy or server in between. With each request MET Norway receives the rounded coordinates, your IP address (as any web server does), and Alison’s User-Agent string (the app name and version with our business contact, support@nightorders.app). MET Norway keeps the IP address and rounded coordinates in its access logs in its own data center in Oslo, Norway, in the EEA, and handles them under its privacy statement. If you are outside Norway, this means that information is transferred to and processed in Norway. Night Orders Studio LLC does not receive or store your location. No journal rows are sent. The values are a model forecast, an estimate, not a measurement at your location, and Alison does not diagnose anything from them. Alison keeps a humidity band and, when your previous weather tap was about 1.5 to 6 hours earlier, a pressure-change band, with the rounded place in your journal on your phone, not the exact readings. To work out that change, Alison keeps only the most recent pressure value on your phone, and replaces it at the next tap. A forecast is reused for about 30 minutes instead of asking again, and Alison never fetches weather in the background. Weather from MET Norway may be unavailable. When it is, Alison shows that the weather feed is unavailable and keeps no new weather sample. Alison 1.2 does not look up pollen or air quality, does not use Nominatim or OpenStreetMap, and does not send a ZIP code. Earlier entries from Alison 1.1 came from Open-Meteo.com and are credited as earlier samples when shown. Deny location and the journal still works.

Update checkAt most once a day, Alison asks Apple’s iTunes lookup for the newest version. The request goes to Apple and carries only the app’s bundle ID and your phone’s region code. Like any request, it shows your network address to Apple. It carries no journal data, and Night Orders Studio LLC does not receive it.
NotesOff by default. A note shows only when the same things have shown up together three times. Not a forecast. No daily nag.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.
KeepThrough Apple: 1-month free trial for new subscribers, then $0.99/month (local price varies). Journal readable without Keep. After your first 24 hours, new Start, Food, and Health pull need Keep. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.

Optional Notes look at diary facts you already logged—foods, weather samples (pressure-change and humidity bands in Alison 1.2; earlier Open-Meteo samples from Alison 1.1), sleep, meds, environment—and only after the same pairing shows up three times. That is what has shown up together, not a forecast and not a cause. Those observations stay on your phone.

Changes, 2026-10-08: In Alison 1.2, weather forecasts now come from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, Oslo, Norway) instead of Apple Weather. Your phone sends the rounded location, your IP address, and Alison’s User-Agent directly to MET Norway. Night Orders Studio LLC receives none of it. Apple is no longer a weather recipient. We added the MET Norway credit. The Alison 1.1 section is unchanged.
