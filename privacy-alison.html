Alison privacy

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Alison is a personal journal from Night Orders. No Alison account. No ads. No analytics. No chat. Alison is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition.

Your journal lives on your iPhone and in iCloud Key-Value Storage with your Apple account, so it comes back on a new phone signed in to that same Apple account. Apple stores that copy. We do not.

JournalOn your iPhone, and in iCloud Key-Value Storage with your Apple account. Apple stores it. We do not. Rows are never sent to a Night Orders server.
EraseErase in the app blanks the book on every phone using this Apple account. Deleting the app removes the on-phone copy; a later install on the same Apple account can bring the iCloud copy back unless you erased it.
SizeApple limits Key-Value Storage to about 1 MB. A normal journal fits. A book over the limit stays on the phone; Alison does not cut it down.
Apple WatchOptional. Start and intensity (1–10) move between your paired iPhone and Watch only.
Apple HealthOptional read on iPhone of food, water, and sleep already on the phone, as a journal overlay, not a diagnosis. Deny still works. A miss shows “did not connect.” Alison never writes to Health.
LocationOptional, When In Use, only to look up local weather, pollen, and pressure from Open-Meteo. Deny and the journal still works.
KeepOptional subscription through Apple. It comes back with Restore Purchases, not with the journal.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used.
