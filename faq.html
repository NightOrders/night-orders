Alison

What is Alison?

An on-device headache journal. Not a medical device. Not a forecast. Notes are Off by default, warn-only, not a medical alarm.

Keep?

Keep is a 30-day free trial, then $0.99/month through Apple. Journal stays readable without it. New Start, Food, and Health pull wait on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, those services end. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.

Watch, Health, food?

Optional. Watch: start and intensity, same journal. Health: optional read of food, water, and sleep already on the device. Open Food Facts is live search. Deny still works.

Pack Run

What is Pack Run?

Are dogs at this park. Nearby parks when location is on. Check-in lasts 90 minutes, then it is erased.

Does it follow me?

Location is When In Use, parks only. No Always. No GPS track, photos, or dog names on the occupancy wire. No account.

Keep?

Keep is a 30-day free trial, then $0.99/month through Apple. Park list stays. Check-in waits on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, check-in ends with it. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
