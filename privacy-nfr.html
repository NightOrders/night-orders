North Front Range privacy

The record stays with you.

North Front Range is an original survival game on the Front Range foothills. No account. No ads. No analytics SDK. No chat.

This is a game. Warmth, hunger, fire, and shelter are game systems. It is not a medical app and not outdoor safety advice. Do not use it for real navigation or rescue.

AccountNone.
ProgressOn your device. Notes and pins you leave in the game stay on this phone.
LocationNot used for real-world tracking. The map is a game world.
Health / MedicalNot used. No diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent claims.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.

Deleting the app removes local progress. We cannot restore it after that.

Support: support@nightorders.app.
