Pack Run privacy

The record stays with you.

Effective 27 August 2026. Night Orders. Independent developer. Bundle com.packrun.app. Live page: https://nightorders.app/privacy-pack-run.html

One job: are dogs at this park. No account. No chat. No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

You check in from your phone. Parks don't share with each other, and nobody owns a park.

AccountNone.
Dog name / photoOn your phone. Camera and photos are optional. Names and photos stay on the phone.
LocationWhen In Use, optional, parks only. No background tracking. No Always. GPS stays on the phone.
Check-inPark and count only. Lasts 90 minutes, then it's gone. Check in again if you're still at the park.
Keep30-day free trial, then $0.99 / month through Apple. Park list stays without Keep. Check-in waits on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, check-in ends with it. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. GDPR / CCPA: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.

Leave clears your check-in now. Deleting the app removes the dogs on this phone. We cannot restore them after that.

Support: support@nightorders.app.
