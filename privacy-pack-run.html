Pack Run privacy

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Pack Run is a dog-park companion from Night Orders. No Night Orders password. No ads. No analytics. No chat.

Your dataYour dogs, photos and preferences stay on this phone (and your paired Apple Watch). There is no account, no iCloud sync and no export.
Photos and cameraDog photos and the camera stay on this phone and are not sent.
Live park presenceCounts, not names. Check-in sends the park, the number of dogs, the time and a random per-install code (not your name or your Apple Account) to our live-board server. The row is erased after 90 minutes; Leave deletes it now. Live presence is not kept as lasting history and is not a permanent Night Orders profile of who was at the park.
LocationWhile Using for nearby parks. No Always. No background track. Deny Location and a short park list still works. Not sold. Not used for ads. If you allow location (While Using), Pack Run sends your coordinates to the OpenStreetMap-based services Overpass and Photon to find nearby dog parks. The coordinates go to overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems and photon.komoot.io. These services, and our live-board server (hosted on Cloudflare), can see your network address.
