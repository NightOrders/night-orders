Pack Run privacy

The record stays with you.

Effective 27 August 2026. Night Orders. Independent developer. Bundle com.packrun.app. Live page: https://nightorders.app/privacy-pack-run.html

One function: are dogs at this park. No account. No chat. No ads. No analytics SDK. No tracking.

Users check in. They do not host. One Cloudflare host holds occupancy. Each park is its own room. A park does not see another park. Nobody owns a park. Nobody runs a server on their phone.

AccountNone.
Dog name / photoOn your device. Camera and photos are optional. They are not sent on the occupancy wire.
LocationWhen In Use, optional, parks only. No background tracking. No Always.
Check-inHashed device + park + pack + time. Lasts 90 minutes, then erased. Re-check-in if still at the park.
Cap80 check-ins per park. Oldest evicted when a park is full. Other parks do not share that cap.
Occupancy hostOne host: pack-run-occupancy.s7jvx9zpbk.workers.dev. Users check in. They do not host. Ask with no park list and the board is empty. If the host is down, occupancy is your device only. The app does not invent a crowd.
Keep30-day free trial, then $0.99 / month through Apple. Park list stays without Keep. Check-in waits on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, check-in ends with it. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used. GDPR / CCPA: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for ads.

Leave deletes the row now. Deleting the app removes the local pack. We cannot restore it after that.

Support: support@nightorders.app.
