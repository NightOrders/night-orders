Pack Run privacy

Data is beautiful, and it is yours.

Pack Run is a dog-park companion from Night Orders. Your dogs list, Keep, and preferences stay device and account only. No Night Orders password. No ads. No analytics. No chat.

AccountYour Apple ID only, for private iCloud when sync is available. No Night Orders password.
Dogs / Keep / preferencesDevice and account only. Export/Import first. Optional private iCloud (same Apple ID) when sync is available.
Live park presenceCounts, not names. Time-boxed erase (ninety minutes, then gone). Leave deletes your live row now. Live presence is not kept as lasting history and is not a permanent Night Orders profile of who was at the park.
Export / ImportMove your owner-owned Pack data between devices you control. Live park presence is not exported as lasting history.
Private iCloudWhen sync is available. Same Apple ID. Private to you. For owner-owned data only—not live park presence.
LocationOnly as required for park features you choose; not sold; not used for ads.
Sale / ads / IDFANot used.
