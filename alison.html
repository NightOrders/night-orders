What it is

Start when it begins. Stop when it ends. Log meals, water, and sleep as they happen. Journal is what you already wrote.

Observational pairs with migraine days (foods, weather, sleep, meds, env). Three repeats. A warning is what has shown up together, not a forecast. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent migraine or any condition.

Ingredient-level food log (Open Food Facts). You can override any hint and delete any row.

Keep

Paid download $0.99. Keep is a 30-day free trial, then $0.99/month through Apple. Journal readable without Keep. New Start, Food, and Health pull wait on Keep. When the trial or Keep ends, those services end. Restore on the Keep screen. Cancel in Apple Subscriptions.

Optional Apple Watch marks start and intensity. Optional Apple Health reads food, water, and sleep already on the device. Turn On All if you want that overlay. Deny still works. The journal still works.
